# Operating model

## Mission

Create a repeatable system that converts high-pressure operational work into consistent execution, durable learning, and visible accountability.

## Roles

| Role | Accountability |
| --- | --- |
| Executive owner | Confirms business priority, approves tradeoffs, removes blockers. |
| Program lead | Owns cadence, quality bar, metrics, and stakeholder alignment. |
| Incident commander | Runs live execution, decision rhythm, and dependency tracking. |
| Technical owner | Validates evidence, root cause, mitigation, and corrective actions. |
| Communications lead | Maintains stakeholder narrative, executive summaries, and updates. |

## Decision rights

- Severity and business impact are confirmed by the program lead and executive owner.
- Technical root cause is owned by the technical owner.
- Customer-facing commitments require a named owner and date.
- Corrective actions are not closed until evidence is attached.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Review rhythm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time to structure | Time from intake to documented owner, impact, action plan, and cadence. | Weekly |
| Decision latency | Time blocked waiting for a required decision. | Weekly |
| Action aging | Open corrective actions by age and owner. | Weekly |
| Repeat signal | Recurrence of similar themes across incidents or escalations. | Monthly |
| Executive readiness | Percent of updates that meet the summary quality bar. | Monthly |

## Quality bar

A package is complete when it explains what happened, why it matters, what is being done, who owns each action, when the next decision is due, and how closure will be verified.
