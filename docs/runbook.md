# Runbook

## Intake

1. Capture trigger, customer or stakeholder impact, severity, and deadline.
2. Assign an accountable owner and backup.
3. Identify known facts, unknowns, dependencies, and immediate risks.
4. Establish the update cadence and communication channel.

## Execution rhythm

| Moment | Action |
| --- | --- |
| First 15 minutes | Confirm scope, owners, severity, and current mitigation. |
| First hour | Publish initial brief with facts, risks, next steps, and asks. |
| Every cadence cycle | Update decisions, blockers, action status, and customer impact. |
| Stabilization | Confirm measurable recovery and transition from response to learning. |
| Closure | Document evidence, lessons, corrective actions, and governance follow-up. |

## Escalation triggers

- No clear owner for a critical workstream.
- Dependency blocked beyond the agreed decision window.
- Customer impact increases or becomes reputational.
- Technical facts conflict across teams.
- Corrective action dates slip without an accepted mitigation.

## Closeout checklist

- Impact summary is factual and time-bounded.
- Root cause and contributing factors are separated.
- Corrective actions have owners, dates, and validation evidence.
- Communication artifacts are archived.
- Repeat-risk signal is routed to governance review.
